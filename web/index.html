opensurgbot: an open-source surgical robot platform

Overview

Surgical robotics is a rapidly growing research field which combines latest advancements in a variety of domains to enhance surgical procedures and improve patient outcomes. The da Vinci Research Kit (dVRK) open-source initiative and the community surrounding it play a crucial role in advancing the state of surgical robotics. Compatible with the dVRK, the SurRoL framework provides a simulation environment suited for training reinforcement learning models for surgical applications.

Acquiring the dVRK is not easy and expensive, we propose opensurgbot as a cost-effective alternative to allow testing SurRoL-trained models in real-world scenarios.
